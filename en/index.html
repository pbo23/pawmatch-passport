Features

What you can do with PaWow

From the first meeting in the park to the yearly check-up: everything about your pet in one place.

💞

Matching

Find playmates or a mate for your dog or cat nearby. Filter by breed and distance, and chat with the owner once it clicks.

🩺

Health record

Vaccinations, medication and vet visits in one place, with reminders on your phone.

🔳

QR vet passport

Your vet scans a QR code and instantly sees vaccinations, the microchip number and past visits. The clinic can send the invoice back through the same link.

🐕

Walk together

See which dogs are out for a walk nearby and join them. Your location is only shared when you start a walk yourself.

📸

Feed

Share photos of your pet and see what other owners post. Like a profile? Send a like right away.

📍

Services nearby

Vets, groomers and pet shops near you, on the map.

🛍️

Product catalog

Food, toys and care products from web shops such as bol and Zooplus, matched to your pet. You order from the shop as usual.

🏠

Adoption

Animals from nearby shelters looking for a new home.

Health

The vet passport, always at hand

Vaccinations, medication, the microchip number and vet visits: PaWow keeps it all in one place, even when the paper passport is in a drawer at home.

  • Reminders for the next vaccination, check-up or medication, right on your phone.
  • A QR link for the vet: valid for 7 days and revocable at any time.
  • The clinic sends the invoice back through the same link, so you can forward it to your insurer.
Catalog

Supplies for your pet, without endless searching

The catalog brings together food, toys, accessories and care products from web shops such as bol and Zooplus, matched to the kind of pet you have.

  • Filtered by species: for dogs, for cats or everything.
  • Price, discount and delivery time at a glance. Prices are refreshed several times a day.
  • You order and pay at the shop itself, with its service and return policy.

Affiliate links

Open about affiliate links

PaWow earns from affiliate links, not from your data. Here is how it works:

  • bol partner programmePaWow takes part in the bol partner programme. Products and prices in the catalog come from bol, and ‘Buy’ takes you to bol.com. PaWow is an independent app and is not part of bol.
  • Commission, no mark-upIf you buy something through a link in our catalog, the shop pays PaWow a small commission. You pay nothing extra.
  • You buy from the shopWe don't sell anything ourselves. Ordering, payment, delivery and returns are handled by the shop, under its terms.
  • The shop's price countsWe refresh prices several times a day, but the price on the shop's website always prevails.
  • Clearly labelledAffiliate links are labelled as such in the app, and we don't pass your personal data to the shop.
  • Honest adviceWe write our articles ourselves. A commission doesn't change what we recommend.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

When will PaWow be in the App Store?

We are testing the app on TestFlight with a small group of owners. As soon as PaWow is in the App Store, you'll find the link on this page.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet: for now PaWow is iPhone-only.

Which languages is the app available in?

Dutch, English and Russian.

Which animals is PaWow for?

Dogs, cats, birds, rabbits and other pets. Walking together is for dogs only.

Who can see my pet's data?

Your pet's name, breed and photo are visible to other users in matching, in the feed and on the walks map. Medical records, documents and messages are visible only to you and to whoever you chat with or share a QR link with.

Does PaWow sell products itself?

No. The catalog links to web shops, where you order and pay. Read how affiliate links work.
