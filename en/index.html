Your data stays yours

  • Stored in data centers in the EU.
  • Medical records are visible only to you, and to the vet if you share a QR link.
  • Others only see your pet's location rounded.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Delete your account and we erase everything, including your pets and messages.
Read the full privacy policy

How PaWow makes money

With affiliate links, not with your data. If you buy something through the catalog, the shop (bol or Zooplus) pays PaWow a small commission. You pay nothing extra.

More about affiliate links
  • bol affiliate program PaWow takes part in the bol affiliate program. Products and prices in the catalog come from bol, and ‘Buy’ takes you to bol.com. PaWow is an independent app and not part of bol.
  • Zooplus via Awin PaWow takes part in the Zooplus affiliate program through the Awin network. ‘Buy’ on a Zooplus product takes you to zooplus.nl. PaWow is not part of Zooplus or Awin.
  • You buy from the store We sell nothing ourselves. Ordering, paying, delivery and returns are between you and the shop, under their terms.
  • The store's price counts We refresh prices several times a day, but the price on the shop's site always applies.
  • Clearly labeled Affiliate links are labeled as such in the app, and we never pass personal data to the store.
  • Honest advice We write our articles ourselves. A commission does not change what we recommend.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get into the beta?

The beta runs on TestFlight, Apple's app for test versions. Right now it is invite-only. Sign up above and we will email you as soon as you can join or PaWow is in the App Store.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet: for now PaWow is iPhone only, with iOS 17 or later.

Which languages does the app support?

Dutch, English and Russian. You can switch inside the app.

Which animals is PaWow for?

Dogs, cats, birds, rabbits and other pets. Walking together is for dogs only.

Who can see my pet's data?

Other users see your pet's name, breed and photo in matching, the feed and on the walks map. Medical records, documents and messages are visible only to you, plus whoever you chat with or share a QR link with.

Does PaWow sell products itself?

No. The catalog links to shops such as bol and Zooplus, where you order and pay. Read how affiliate links work.

Be there when PaWow opens

One email when the beta opens or PaWow is in the App Store. Nothing more.
