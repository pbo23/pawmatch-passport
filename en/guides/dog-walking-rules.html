Walking your dog in the Netherlands: leash rules, clean-up and off-leash areas

4 min readUpdated on 14 October 2026 By the PaWow editors

Where can your dog run free, and where does it have to be on a leash? In the Netherlands that is mostly up to your municipality. These are the rules you will run into in practice.

Every municipality has its own rules

Municipalities set out their dog rules in the local by-laws (Algemene Plaatselijke Verordening, APV), often with a map of dog zones. A common rule is that dogs must be on a leash in built-up areas, except in designated off-leash areas. Search your municipality's website for ‘honden’ or ‘hondenkaart’ to see what applies where you live.

Clean-up duty

In most municipalities you have to pick up after your dog, and often you must also carry a poop bag. If you don't, you risk a fine.

Off-leash areas

In an off-leash area your dog may run free, as long as it comes when called. Call your dog to you when children, cyclists or other dogs approach, and pick up after it here too.

Nature areas and the breeding season

In many woods and nature areas dogs must be on a leash, often with separate off-leash zones. During the breeding season, roughly from mid-March to mid-July, stricter rules apply in many places to protect birds that nest on the ground. Check the signs at the entrance or the website of the site manager, such as Staatsbosbeheer or Natuurmonumenten.

On the beach

On many beaches dogs are not welcome during the day in the summer season, or only on a leash; outside the season more is often allowed. This also differs per municipality, and sometimes per beach entrance.

Dog tax

Many municipalities charge a dog tax, but not all of them: some have abolished it. Register your dog if your municipality charges the tax.

What to bring

  • A leash, even in an off-leash area: you never know when you will need it.
  • Poop bags, enough for the whole walk.
  • Water and a collapsible bowl on warm days.
  • Treats, to practise recall.
  • In the dark: a light-up collar or a small lamp, so your dog is clearly visible.

Hot weather

Asphalt and paving can get so hot in the sun that your dog burns its paw pads. Put your hand on the ground for five seconds: if it is too hot for you, it is too hot for your dog. On warm days walk early in the morning or in the evening, and choose shade and grass.

This article gives general information. The rules differ per municipality and per nature area; check what applies to you on the website of your municipality or the site manager.
